PUBLIC-BIAS RESEARCH — 2026-11-22 (America/New_York). Private research; raw odds stay in the bot store, nothing committed.

QUESTION: Do public-money patterns (overs, stars, popular teams, prime time, favorites, line moves, soft vs sharp books) bend
closing prices enough that a de-biased probability beats the books' no-vig probability?

DATA
- Player props (passing/rushing/receiving yards, receptions), pushes dropped, deduped per player-week-market:
  EndzoneAPI history (with open line + real over/under prices) and EndzoneAPI game probabilities (no-vig fair prob, avg prices),
  plus repo prop-closes.json (PropLine CC BY sample, 2026 weeks 2-3, soft-book consensus and Pinnacle no-vig; -110 assumed for ROI).
  2024 n=239, 2025 n=1117, 2026 n=1093. (Count differs slightly from the earlier 1,453 EZ set because of dedupe key and the added PropLine rows.)
- Game lines: repo closing-lines.json (nflverse, CC BY) 2015-2026 regular season, closing spread/total/moneyline WITH prices; kickoff day/time
  from nflverse games.csv. No opening lines exist in this file.
- Popularity proxies (free, point-in-time safe): prior-season fantasy PPR rank from nflverse stats in repo (star = top-12 QB/TE, top-24 RB/WR);
  1st-round draft pick (nflverse players.csv); a-priori "public team" list DAL GB PIT KC SF PHI (fixed before testing); prime time = Thu/Mon or night kickoff.
- NOT usable yet: repo price-history.json starts 2026-11-19 (essentially no graded games); book-props snapshots have no graded results.

METHOD
- Each signal = one feature x. De-biased prob p' = logistic(logit(noVig) + b*x). b fit (ridge lambda=5, needs >=30 active training rows,
  else b=0) ONLY on weeks strictly before the scored week (week-based walk-forward; games train from 2015, props from 2024).
- Score: Brier of p' vs books' no-vig, per season, with game-clustered bootstrap 95% CI on the difference.
- Signal group: actual event rate vs mean no-vig implied, 95% CI, z.
- ROI (diagnostic only, NOT a pick or edge claim): flat bet on the anti-public side in the group at real prices (EZ history/nflverse),
  EZ avg prices, or -110 (PropLine); and flat bet following the walk-forward p' side.
- Pass rule: p' Brier < no-vig Brier in BOTH 2025 and 2026.
- Multiple testing: 18 tests run (9 prop, 9 game). Bonferroni family-wise 5% threshold |z| >= 2.99.

  it was significantly WORSE in 2024, and it loses to the books pooled over 2016-2026. With 18 tests, about one noise "pass" is expected.

- No signal reached the Bonferroni threshold (largest |z| = 2.95, P2 stars in 2024, and that went the OPPOSITE way: star overs hit MORE).
- Overall props leaned UNDER in 2025 (over 0.466 vs 0.498 implied, z=-2.17) and 2026 (0.476 vs 0.496, z=-1.37) but OVER in 2024
  (0.552 vs 0.500); the walk-forward under-lean still did not beat no-vig Brier. Under-bets at real prices were ~breakeven-minus-vig.

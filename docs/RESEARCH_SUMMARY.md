# Visual research priorities

Implemented 2026-11-17. Whole-game and open-ended opportunity questions now lead with **The research read**, a responsive four-market summary. Exact-side/line questions retain their own decision and charts; the cross-market summary remains in Compare the markets.

## What is ranked

The system ranks observed evidence, never model win probabilities, expected value, historical hit rates or hypothetical returns. A candidate needs a usable exact quote; blocked, future and over-18-hour observations are excluded. Coverage checks show an opposing quote, multiple books, an observation within two hours, dated history and lineup clarity. Props add recorded workload context.

For the history check, game markets need at least three available final games for both teams; props need at least five recorded production appearances. Workload needs three appearances and an observed recent mean. These are transparent research-coverage heuristics, not statistical sufficiency thresholds or evidence of predictive skill.

Reviewable candidates have paired prices, the history coverage, a quote within two hours and no flagged lineup/role conflict. Unknown starters stay unknown rather than becoming a health confirmation. Such candidates take priority over stale or incomplete options. Within that group, fixed weights are opposing price 4, multiple books 2, recency 1, history 2, prop workload 1 and explicitly confirmed lineup context 1. No parameters are tuned to forecast outcomes.

Equal scores remain tied across markets and selections. Different selections use a neutral stable ordering for a representative quote; a higher payout is considered only between identical selections. Representative quotes are explicitly not preferred sides. All actions remain Pass/research only because no model has demonstrated a betting edge.

## Why and opposing evidence

The full comparison includes dated raw records relevant to the market: prior margins for spreads, separate combined-score histories for totals, prior W/L/T for moneylines, and production/workload history for props. These records explain what evidence is available; they do not causally validate a bet or adjust a forecast.

The summary highlights stale quotes, missing opposing prices or histories, flagged quarterback/lineup concerns and unknown participation. If nothing clears the research checks, it gives a clear pass and describes the needed refresh. Coverage bars count available checks and are never labeled as confidence or win probability.

Focused follow-up links preserve the exact event date and market. The existing charts, source checks, explicit-selection answers and refresh/expiry behavior remain intact. No extra inference or odds requests, quota changes, forecast versions or model adoption are introduced.

## Verification

Tests cover unique priorities, ties, stale multi-book versus fresh usable evidence, absent/blocked/future quotes, workload support, lineup conflicts, invariance to forecast optimism, safe summary validation, phone layouts with enlarged text, grounded follow-ups and retaining the requested selection's own answer.
